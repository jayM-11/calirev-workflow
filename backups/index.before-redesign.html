CALIFORNIA EMISSIONS
Manual source review · no automatic compliance decisionNot every aftermarket part needs an Executive Order. First assess whether the part affects emissions controls, then check the exact vehicle application and conditions. Record what the source says; unresolved cases stay in follow-up.

  1. Identify whether the part changes or affects an emissions-control system; do not assume every part needs an EO.
  2. Search the official CARB database by EO or part details. Match the exact year, make, model, engine/test group and installation conditions shown in the source.
  3. Save the official source link, note the conclusion and any limits, and record the reviewer and date.
  4. If the source, fitment or applicability is unclear, mark follow-up. A missing search result is not a compliance decision.
